Chocolate Cake Recipe

A simple and delicious chocolate cake.

Cooking Time: 45 minutes

Warning: Be careful while handling the hot oven!

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup butter

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Mix flour, sugar and cocoa powder.
  3. Add eggs, milk and butter.
  4. Mix everything well.
  5. Pour the batter into a cake pan.
  6. Bake for 45 minutes.
Nutrition Facts

Calories: 350 kcal

Protein: 5g

Fat: 15g
